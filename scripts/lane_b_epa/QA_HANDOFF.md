## Authoritative joins and orphan handling

- **ECHO:** native FRS `REGISTRY_ID` is an identity within the federal regulatory catalog, not proof of a single property. Counts/penalty summaries within the wide ECHO Exporter are **summaries**, not independently verified individual violation event rows. Source uses `FAC_LAT`, `FAC_LONG` when reported.
- **RCRAInfo:** official documented link keys are **`ID_NUMBER` + `ACTIVITY_LOCATION`** (both required); report tables, evaluation/enforcement/violation/SNC history are separate. The complete official profile found **11,212 source rows** without an exact two-key match in `RCRA_FACILITIES.csv`: 1,677 enforcements, 5,501 evaluations, 1,519 NAICS, 2,515 violations, 0 SNC-history records. Those remain individually indexed/unlinked, **never force linked** using names or handler ID alone. Even an exact same-regulator match does not independently confirm a physical insured property.
- **TRI:** exact native `2. TRIFD` is the reporting facility identity; `36. DOC_CTRL_NUM` and the chemical/reporting year distinguish reports; `3. FRS ID` is a **candidate** external crosswalk, not automatic site mapping. Full five-year source provenance by year at `validation/tri_2020_2024.md`.
- **RMP:** public FRS programme association records support exact native `PGM_SYS_ID`, `REGISTRY_ID`, `OBJECTID` lookup. They do **not** include sensitive RMP/OCA plans, chemicals, release scenarios or losses. EPA access restrictions must be respected; authorized non-OCA releases would require a separate reviewed source.

## Measured source semantics, date anomalies and safe numerical interpretation

The complete official-source field profiler [run 39980093306](https://github.com/jhennessy-pharm3r/FacilityTest/actions/runs/39980093306) read **all source CSV members**, not sample fixtures. Its archived private aggregate profiles include actual column lists, source-native key duplicates, reported date years, state/territory strings, geospatial bounds and reported status codes. Source archives were last modified **2026-10-18** according to ECHO/RCRA HTTP Last-Modified headers; this is NOT an effective date for every facility observation.

- ECHO exporter `FAC_COMPLIANCE_STATUS` (all **3,229,311 raw rows**, including 21,303 keyless quarantined records): `No Violation Identified` 2,227,570; `Significant Violation` 22,200; `Violation Identified` 52,660; `Violation` 2,085; `Inactive` 288,698; `Unknown` 15,184; blank 620,914. Independently, `FAC_SNC_FLG` contains **3,229,311 'N'** values, despite multiple status strings including Significant Violation. **Do not infer no significant noncompliance from the lone flag or conflate statuses with actual individual enforcement events.** ECHO `FAC_DATE_LAST_INFORMAL_ACTION` and `FAC_DATE_LAST_INFORMAL_ACT_ST` contain **five pre-1900 and nine post-2026** year values each (including 0001 and 8888); `FAC_DATE_LAST_INSPECTION` contains a year-1016 sentinel and state inspection mirrors it. Original strings remain intact. The read-only adapter explicitly rejects implausible dates as *source out of plausible range*, not known event dates.
- RCRA event-table date oddities in all source rows: `ENFORCEMENT_ACTION_DATE` has **7 pre-1900 source year values**; `EVALUATION_START_DATE` **17**; `DATE_VIOLATION_DETERMINED` **37**; `ACTUAL_RTC_DATE` **2**. `SCHEDULED_COMPLIANCE_DATE` contains **4 pre-1900** and **110 post-2026** values, which may include future regulatory schedules and publisher placeholders—not historical violations. `YRMONTH` represents **year-month**, not a precise enforcement date. RCRA evaluation `FOUND_VIOLATION`: **382,796 'Y' / 777,825 'N' / 10,119 'U' / 23 'R'** source table rows. RCRA `VIOSNC_HISTORY.SNC_FLAG` Y appears on **376,449** *history rows*, not 376,449 distinct sites or events.
- TRI 2024 raw cohort reports **68,567 Form R** and **8,728 Form A** entries; 232 chemical-report rows carry EPA's PFAS field value `YES`. They do not represent 232 independently verified contaminated properties. Grams-unit reports (721) are distinguished from pounds-unit reports (76,574) at observation level. New `findings.py` retains exact reported decimal strings and raw units and, for **a single chemical observation**, separately computes exact kg with Grams×0.001 / Pounds×0.45359237. The parser explicitly rejects nonfinite, implausible, negative or unknown-unit values; no cross-chemical/unit or insurance-loss aggregation occurs. RCRA separate enforcement monetary columns are not auto-summed; ECHO penalties are aggregate source summaries rather than paid claims.
- Publisher-reported coordinate bounding boxes from *all raw coordinate-bearing records*: ECHO latitude **-14.356083 to 74.256738** and longitude **-180 to 179.28679**; RCRA facility latitude **-14.33666 to 80** and longitude **-179.28679 to 145.809342**; TRI 2024 latitude **-14.273281 to 70.245664** and longitude **-170.702961 to 145.730981**. These correspond to publisher coordinate extents, **not global, parcel, coverage or position-accuracy guarantees**. The ECHO exporter includes **62,208 blank state codes** in all raw rows and special territory/foreign code strings; RCRA includes state, territory, tribal and foreign/special codes. Preserve them rather than forcing continental-US state locations.

Source dates, compliance summaries, substances, penalty amounts and reported coordinates must remain clearly separated from independently verified physical plant, insured property, loss, closure, coverage or payout findings.
